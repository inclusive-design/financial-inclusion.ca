**FIRM (Financial Inclusion and Resilience Movement)** is a growing community-led movement connecting organisations, institutions, researchers, and individuals across Canada who are working to strengthen financial inclusion and community resilience.

Many communities experience barriers that limit access to financial knowledge, services, opportunities, and support. At the same time, community organisations often face challenges related to capacity, sustainability, administrative burden, and access to specialised expertise. FIRM helps address these interconnected challenges through collaboration, shared learning, and collective action.

Through the **Shared Services Hub**, **Knowledge Hub**, **Community of Practice**, and **community-led governance**, FIRM helps organisations and communities build the capacity, connections, and infrastructure needed to create lasting change.

Whether you are a community organisation, financial institution, researcher, policymaker, or someone with lived experience, FIRM provides opportunities to connect, contribute, and help build a more inclusive financial future.

## What FIRM Does

### Shared Services Hub

Helping organizations strengthen capacity by exploring shared resources, expertise, tools, and services that reduce operational burdens and support long-term sustainability.

### Knowledge Hub

Mobilizing community-generated knowledge, practical tools, research, and resources that help advance financial inclusion and organizational resilience.

### Community of Practice

Creating opportunities for peer learning, collaboration, networking, and collective problem-solving across communities and sectors.

### Community-Led Governance

Supporting inclusive decision-making and ensuring that disability, Deaf, and intersectional communities remain at the centre of FIRM's future direction.
